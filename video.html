📘 Help & Shortcuts

Recording
  • Use Record Lecture for full studio
  • Choose camera, mic, quality
  • Floating webcam overlay during screen capture
  • Pause/resume supported
Editing
  • Click clips in timeline to select
  • Drag edges to trim
  • S split at playhead
  • Del delete selected
Keyboard
  • Space Play/Pause
  • Ctrl+Z Undo
  • Ctrl+Y Redo
  • Ctrl+S Save
  • Esc Close modal
Export
  • WebM is the most compatible browser format
  • MP4 availability depends on browser
  • Use 1080p for most lectures
Privacy
  • All data stays in your browser
  • Camera/mic require permission
  • Recording indicator always visible
  • No data is uploaded to any server
Recommendations
  • Use latest Chrome or Edge
  • HTTPS or localhost for full features
  • Close other tabs for better performance
